From long video to viral clip in 4 steps

No timeline editing, no software to install. Paste a link and let the pipeline do the rest.

01

Paste a link or upload your video

Drop in a YouTube, Zoom or podcast URL, or upload an MP4/MOV directly. We fetch the full-length video for you.

02

Our AI finds the best moments

Word-level transcription feeds a GLM-powered analysis engine (z.ai) that scores every moment for hook strength, emotion and shareability.

03

Get vertical clips with auto-captions

Shotstack renders each clip in 9:16 with animated word-by-word captions, reframed to keep the speaker centered.

04

Schedule & auto-post

Queue clips to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels — ClipForge publishes them at the perfect time.

Everything you need to go viral

One pipeline from raw footage to scheduled posts — built for podcasters, streamers and marketing teams.

AI Viral Moment Detection

A GLM-powered model (z.ai) reads your full transcript and surfaces the moments most likely to blow up, ranked by virality score.

Auto Word-by-Word Captions

Animated captions highlight every word as it's spoken — with your brand color #FF5D1C as the default highlight.

Smart 9:16 Reframing

Every clip is cropped to vertical and scaled to 1080×1920, keeping faces and key visuals in frame automatically.

Multi-Platform Scheduling

Plan a week of content in one sitting. Delayed jobs publish each clip to TikTok, Reels and Shorts right on schedule.

Custom Caption Styles & Branding

Pick fonts, colors, positions and animation presets — or drop your logo watermark on every clip you render.

Fast Cloud Rendering

Everything renders in the cloud via the Shotstack API. No downloads, no plugins, no GPU fan noise.
